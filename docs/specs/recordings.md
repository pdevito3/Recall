# Spec: audio

## 1. Scope

A person sends audio to the server, in slices, and the server turns it into text in the Raw of a Session. The server stores the audio until the text is saved. A word must never vanish: audio that did not become text stays stored, and the person can see why.

This spec covers the `Recording`, its slices, the jobs that make its text, the speech model, the cloud engine, and the audio files. It leaves out each screen (R12) and the connection of a provider (R9 owns it, see [model-providers.md](../backend/model-providers.md)).

Plan section R11 gives the reasons.

## 2. Entities and states

The `recordings` table is not in the sync feed. The text of a Recording reaches each device through the feed of its Session, and the upload state is of use only to the device that uploads. The table has its own check that refuses a row of a deleted owner.

| Entity | Root of | Fields that a user sets | States |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Recording` | `RecordingSlice` | client recording ID, format | Receiving, Received, Transcribing, Transcribed, Failed |

| From | To | Method | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receiving | Received | `Recording.Finish` | The owner |
| Received, Transcribing | Transcribing | `Recording.BeginAttempt` | The transcription job |
| Received | Received, with the problem `WaitingForModel` | `Recording.WaitForModel` | The transcription job |
| Transcribing | Received, with the problem `ProviderUnavailable` | `Recording.WaitForProvider` | The cloud transcription job |
| Received, Transcribing | Received, on the local engine | `Recording.HandToLocalEngine` | The cloud transcription job |
| Transcribing | Transcribed | `Recording.Complete` | The transcription job |
| Received, Transcribing | Failed | `Recording.Fail` | The transcription job |
| Failed | Received | `Recording.Retry` | The owner |
| Receiving | Received, or Failed with `NoAudioReceived` | `Recording.EndQuietUpload` (REC-23) | The sweep |
| Received, Transcribing, Transcribed, Failed, after a quiet end | Receiving | `Recording.StoreSlice` (REC-24) | The owner |

`Recording.Complete` gives Failed with `NoWordsFound` when no chunk had words. `Recording.BeginAttempt` gives Failed with `TooManyAttempts` after `MaxAttemptsWithoutProgress` attempts with no saved chunk, or with `ProviderUnavailable` when the provider did not answer the last of them.

| REC-14 | A person can see that the jobs that make text stopped, with what to do. A Recording that waits for its text says so in `problemText`, and its `status` and `problem` stay as they are. | REC-15 | A failure names a cause only when the server has seen it. `DiskFull` and `DiskRefused` need the words of the disk error from ffmpeg. `AudioUnreadable` needs the words that ffmpeg 7.1.3 uses for bad audio, and only this problem tells a person to record again. | REC-16 | The decoded copy of the audio, whole or partial, lasts only while a Recording waits for its text. | REC-17 | An Admin chooses one model provider that takes audio, with its model, for the tenant. A Recording takes the engine of the tenant at its finish, and at a retry after a failure. Anthropic takes no audio, and a choice of it gives 422. | `ChooseTranscriptionEngine`, `Recording.Finish`, `Recording.Retry`, `BeginTranscription`, `SaveTranscriptChunk` | |
| REC-18 | The cloud engine sends the decoded 16 kHz mono WAV, so each format of a client reaches a provider in a format that it takes. A part holds at most 2 minutes, inside the output limit of 2,000 tokens of `gpt-4o-transcribe`, and at most 25,000,000 bytes, the limit of OpenAI and Groq read as decimal MB. A part over the limit ends at the quietest 50 ms in the last 5 s before it, and at least 1 s before the end of the audio, and the next part starts at that sample, so the text of each part joins once, in order, with no gap. A part is saved as a chunk by REC-6, so a lease return sends only the parts after the pointer. An answer that the provider cut at its output limit is not saved, its part goes again at half its length, and the rest of the run keeps that length. | `CloudTranscriptionEngine`, `AudioParts`, `PcmAudio`, `OpenAiCompatibleClient.TranscribeAsync` | Each part is under the limit by its construction. `ProviderLimits.AudioOutputTokens` |
| REC-19 | A provider that refuses fails the Recording with `ProviderRefused`, a bad answer with `ProviderBadAnswer`, and a key that the server cannot read with `ProviderKeyUnreadable`. After `MaxAttemptsWithoutProgress` such tries it fails with `ProviderUnavailable`, and its text tells the person to try again, since no try comes on its own. | REC-21 | With no chosen provider, the local engine makes the text and the audio stays on the server. The engine route says why: no provider takes audio, or none is chosen. A job reads the choice again after each part, so after a change no new part goes to the old provider: the job begins again from the saved text with the new choice, or gives the Recording to the local engine. Only the part in flight at the change ends at the old provider. | `GetTranscriptionEngine`, `BeginTranscription` | |
| REC-22 | No job row, log, span, support bundle, or dashboard page gets a word of a transcript, a byte of audio, or the key of a provider. A failure that a job keeps is a fixed text. | `JobPayloadTests`, `JobSafeTextException`, `ProviderFailureText` | |
| REC-23 | An upload that gets no new slice for 24 hours ends with the slices that came, at the next sweep, at each start and each hour. The slices join in the order of their index, also with a gap, and the text of that audio comes as after a finish. With no slice the Recording fails with `NoAudioReceived`, and a retry of it gives 409. A slice that comes later joins by REC-24. A slice whose file the server lost leaves a gap too, and `problemText` says that part of the audio was lost. The sweep ends each Recording on its own, so one that cannot end logs a warning, waits for the next sweep, and holds no other Recording and not the queue of REC-7. | `Recording.EndQuietUpload`, `RecordingSweep` | |
| REC-24 | A client that kept the rest of the audio can send it after the sweep of REC-23 ended the upload. A slice above the highest slice that the sweep joined opens the Recording again as Receiving, from Received, Transcribing, Transcribed, or Failed. A Recording that ended with `NoAudioReceived` opens again on its first slice. The late slices follow REC-2 and REC-4, and the finish follows REC-3 for the slices after the joined audio only, so a gap that the sweep joined stays a gap. A slice at or under the joined slice gives 409. The finish adds the late slices after the joined audio, and the job goes on from the sample pointer, so only the words of the late part join Raw, after the text that the entry has then by REC-6, and an edit of the person stays. No sample is transcribed twice, but a word that the end of the upload cut can show at the end of the first words and again at the start of the late words, because the text of the first part is not written again. A run that began before the late part does not end the Recording: it begins again from the saved text. A deleted entry gives 404, and a Recording whose audio is gone by REC-5 gives 409. A slice after a finish of the client gets 409. Each join, by the finish or the sweep, writes a new audio file, and only the save that names it in `audio_file` makes it the audio. A step that loses its save deletes only its own new file. After a win it deletes the files that the row no longer names: the old audio, its decoded copy, and the joined slices. So a stale sweep never writes over the audio of a finish, and each read of the audio uses the file that the row names. A slice file goes only after the save that joined it, and the kept audio goes only after the save that marks it gone, so a late slice never loses its file to a run, a sweep, or the removal of the audio. The removal of the kept audio, the delete of the entry, and the sweep take the files that a lost slice write left. A finish that a rival finish beat gives the answer of a repeat. A slice file that the server lost leaves a gap that `problemText` names, and a late part after that gap ends and gets its words. Joined audio that the server lost ends the late finish, and the transcription fails with `AudioMissing`. So a finish never gives 404 for a file. | `Recording.StoreSlice`, `Recording.Finish`, `RecordingWork.JoinAudioAndSaveAsync`, `RecordingFiles.Audio`, `RemoveKeptAudio`, `CompleteTranscription`, `FailTranscription`, `WorkAudio` | `recordings.quiet_end_through_slice`, `recordings.audio_file` |
| REC-25 (later) | A finish can name the taps of New paragraph in `paragraphTapsMs`: the ms of each tap from the start of the audio. The server never refuses them. It sorts them, drops repeats and taps outside 0 to 4 hours, and keeps the first 1,000. The first finish that succeeds stores them. A repeat finish gives the answer of the repeat and keeps the first taps, also when it names others. A late finish (REC-24) after a quiet end stores no taps (REC-31). A Recording with no taps gets its text as before. | `ParagraphTaps.Clean`, `Recording.Finish`, `FinishRecording` | |
| REC-26 | With measured word times, a tap breaks the text at the widest gap between two words whose middle is within 1,000 ms of it or whose silence holds it, or at the gap whose silence is nearest when none is. The earliest gap wins a tie. A break is a boundary between two words and never a character, so it never falls inside a word and never changes one. Two taps in one gap give one break. A tap before the first words of a chunk breaks before them. A tap after the last words, or in a chunk with no words, breaks before the next words. At a cut between two chunks the silence that the chunk sees is mirrored across the cut, so its width is twice that silence, and a tap on either side can take the cut. A tap past the end of the audio gives no break. The times are measured only when the engine gives one span for each word of the text, in order. | `ParagraphBreaks.Of` | |
| REC-27 | In a Recording with taps, each chunk goes on in the last paragraph of Raw, after a space. Only the start of the Recording, a tap, a last block that is not a paragraph, and an edit of Raw since the chunk before start a paragraph, so a chunk never runs into the words of the person. A Recording with no taps, or with lost audio (REC-23), gives Raw as before, byte for byte: each chunk is its own paragraph. A known limit: two Recordings that make text in one Session at once can join a chunk of one to the last paragraph of the other. | `ParagraphBreaks.Of`, `RawTranscript.Append`, `Recording.TakeChunk`, `SaveTranscriptChunk` | |
| REC-28 (later) | Each tap gives its break once. A tap belongs to the one chunk whose samples hold it, and a break that waits for the next words is saved with the sample pointer of REC-6, in the same save. So a lease return, a run that goes on from the pointer, a retry, a repeat of an upload or of a finish, and a hand-over to the other engine add no break twice. A run that goes on from the pointer places the taps after it against its own cuts. | `Recording.TakeChunk`, `Recording.Finish` | `recordings.next_words_start_paragraph` |
| REC-29 | With no measured word times, as on the cloud engine, or when the times of the local engine fail the check of REC-26, each word gets a share of the samples of its chunk by its share of the characters. A tap then breaks the text at the sentence end nearest it within 12 words of its estimated boundary: after a word that ends in `.`, `?`, or `!`, which a closing quote or bracket may follow, or at the start or the end of the chunk. With none in reach it breaks at the estimated boundary. It never falls inside a word and never changes one. The estimate drifts, so a break can land a sentence off. | `ParagraphBreaks.Of` | |
| REC-30 (later) | The web client counts a tap from the start event of the recorder, on the clock of `performance.now()`, at the press of the finger. It stores each tap with the capture before the capture ends. Both finish paths, the upload and the end of a capture on the server, send the taps. | | |
